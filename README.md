# Filafinder

<p align="center">
  <img src="filafinder-logo.svg" alt="Filafinder Logo" width="180" height="180">
</p>

**Filafinder** is an interactive, browser-based decision tool designed to help 3D printing enthusiasts and beginners select the optimal filament for their specific projects.

Instead of navigating long lists of technical specifications, users are guided through a series of intuitive questions regarding their project goals—such as environmental requirements, mechanical stress, aesthetics, and hardware constraints—to find the perfect material.

## Supported Filaments
* **Standard PLA:** The gold standard for ease of printing and visual models.
* **Silk PLA:** High-gloss, metallic sheen, and silky luster finish.
* **Matte PLA:** Smooth, non-reflective finish that hides layer lines.
* **PETG:** Tough workhorse for mechanical brackets and general utility.
* **TPU:** Flexible, highly elastic elastomer for impact absorption.
* **PP:** Ultra-lightweight with fatigue-resistant living hinge capabilities.
* **ASA:** The definitive choice for outdoor UV endurance.
* **PC:** Extreme engineering thermoplastic for heavy loads and high heat.
* **CF/GF Reinforced Versions:** Carbon and glass fiber-infused variants for maximum stiffness and structural rigidity.
